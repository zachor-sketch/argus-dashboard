## Preservation and adaptation

- Existing layout, sidebar, cards, charts, board, search, filters, and six research panels are retained and extended.
- Hebrew is the default application language; English remains selectable. Original analyst narrative is preserved verbatim in source-language blocks to avoid silently changing its meaning; all application controls, headings, labels, warnings and methodology are bilingual.
- The filename of the HTML is not a claim that every embedded object is V10.25. Its later shadow objects are stored separately.
- Original formulas are copied verbatim into a factory with an explicit clock and inputs. The locked replay uses the source lock timestamp. Today's estimates never update the historical dataset.
- Missing verified current quotes produce unavailable value/P&L/weight, not zero. Locked-price and purchase-cost estimates remain explicitly estimates. Historical PIT quotes in the attachments are not silently promoted to live quotes.
- Source scenario expected value and recorded locked expected value are distinct fields and may differ. Both are retained.
- The supplied portfolio overlay supersedes historical sizing for current allocation only: INTU 6–8% target / 12% maximum / DO NOT ADD. Historical source sizing remains visible as historical context.
- Local event, forecast, learning and proof records are append-only; material events create a new dated shadow snapshot linked to the immutable parent. Local unverified outcomes cannot authorize trading.
- Workbook synthetic loops and historical diagnostic results cannot count as clean-forward proof. Blank journal/promise template rows are not fabricated resolved observations.
- No background market feed or event scheduler is claimed. Newly entered events require an explicit evidence source; price movement alone cannot create a material fundamental upgrade.
